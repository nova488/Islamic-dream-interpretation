## Interpretation of Dreaming of a Leopard from the Perspective of Great Islamic Scholars

### Interpretation of Dreaming of a Leopard According to Imam Ja'far al-Sadiq

Imam Ja'far al-Sadiq (AS) considers the interpretation of dreaming of a leopard to have three aspects: first, a powerful and open enemy; second, acquiring wealth and spoils from a powerful person; and third, attaining high status, position, or sovereignty, provided that you overcome the leopard.

### Ibn Sirin's Perspective: The Leopard as a Powerful and Shrewd Enemy

Ibn Sirin views the leopard as a symbol of a wise, intelligent, and resolute enemy. If you fight a leopard in a dream and win, you will triumph over such an enemy; however, if the leopard defeats you, you will suffer a loss or illness.

### Prophet Joseph's (AS) Narrative on Seeing Predators in Dreams

Prophet Joseph (AS) considers seeing predatory animals like leopards to be a sign of encountering tyrannical and powerful kings or rulers, where facing them requires tact, and overcoming them brings honor and greatness.

### Opinions of Jaber Maghrabi and Prophet Daniel (AS)

Prophet Daniel (AS) states that owning or taming a leopard indicates companionship and friendship with a high-ranking but dangerous individual. Jaber Maghrabi also believes that eating leopard meat or owning its skin in a dream means gaining wealth, benefit, and victory in war and competition.

## Examining Different Scenarios of Seeing a Leopard in a Dream

### Seeing a Calm Leopard or Seeing It from a Distance

It indicates the presence of a hidden threat or enemy in your life who is currently harmless, but you must remain cautious of them.

### Being Attacked or Chased by a Leopard

It represents facing major problems, severe anxiety, or the risk of being harmed by a powerful and influential individual.

### Confronting a Leopard Without Being Harmed

It is a sign that you possess great courage and capability in managing crises and facing your fierce competitors.

### Interpretation of Specific Scenarios and Different Species of Leopards

Dreaming of a black panther (leopard) usually symbolizes the deception and trickery of an enemy, while seeing a white or wounded leopard indicates sudden changes in fortune or health status.

### Escaping from a Leopard or Overcoming It

It means deliverance from grief and sorrow, leaving major obstacles behind, and achieving a definitive victory over enemies or rivals in waking life.

### A Leopard Attacking or Biting

It serves as a warning of financial harm, illness, or losing a deal and competition due to your own negligence.
