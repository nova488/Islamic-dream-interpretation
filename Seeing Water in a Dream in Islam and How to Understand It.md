## Interpretation of Seeing Water in a Dream from the Perspective of Great Scholars

* **Ibn Sirin:** He considers seeing clear and abundant water as a sign of longevity, lawful livelihood, and health, while interpreting dark and muddy water as grief, illness, and hardship in life.
* **Imam Sadiq (AS):** He interprets seeing water in a dream in 5 aspects: calamity and temptation, purity of the soul, blessing and livelihood, breakthrough in affairs, and companionship with great people.
* **Prophet Joseph (AS):** He interprets seeing clear and flowing water as a sign of exiting grief and sorrow, deliverance from hardships, and attaining wealth and peace after adversity.

## Different Scenarios of Seeing Water in a Dream

* **Interpretation of dreaming about clear and flowing water:** This scenario is one of the most popular searches and indicates peace of mind, breakthroughs in entangled affairs, healing from illness, and entering a period of life with lawful and worry-free livelihood.
* **Interpretation of dreaming about muddy and dirty water:** Many users are worried about this dream; as it is usually interpreted as a sign of grief and sorrow, dubious wealth, illness, or the existence of turmoil and insecurity in the family and work environment.
* **Interpretation of dreaming about drowning in water:** It is among the very common and stressful dreams, which often indicates the dreamer drowning in material problems, debt, or sinking into sins and negligence of spiritual matters.
* **Interpretation of dreaming about a flood and house inundation:** This search usually occurs when the person is under severe stress in real life. In interpretation, a flood entering the house means the occurrence of sedition, family problems, or the entry of a major and sudden challenge into life.
* **Interpretation of dreaming about drinking water (especially cold water):** Users are usually looking for the pleasant feeling of this dream. Drinking pleasant water means gaining knowledge and wisdom, longevity, and the fulfillment of a need you have been waiting for for a long time.
* **Interpretation of dreaming about swimming in water:** If the swimming is easy, it is a sign of the person's mastery over problems and success on the path to goals; but if the person is struggling, it is interpreted as confronting great hardships in life.
* **Interpretation of dreaming about performing ablution (wudu) or taking a ritual bath (ghusl) with water:** This scenario is highly searched by religious users and symbolizes the purification of the soul, accepted repentance, getting rid of debt and grief, and a fresh and clean start in life.
* **Interpretation of dreaming about pouring water on the ground or splashing water:** This dream is usually interpreted as wasting wealth or energy; however, if splashing water on someone's face is to wake them up or as a joke, it can mean bringing goodness and benefit to that person.
