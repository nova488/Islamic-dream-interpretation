## Dream Interpretation of Seeing Blood from the Perspective of Great Scholars

* **Ibn Sirin:** Seeing blood in a dream is generally a sign of forbidden wealth (Haram money), sin, or grief and financial loss, unless it comes out of a festering wound, which means the warding off of calamity and the improvement of affairs.
* **Imam Sadiq (AS):** Blood in a dream is interpreted in four ways; it is either a sign of forbidden wealth, grief and sorrow, betrayal and lies, or under certain circumstances (such as cupping/Hijama), a sign of good health and getting rid of hardships.
* **Prophet Joseph (AS):** Seeing blood often means deliverance from the bond of sorrow, healing from illness, and the purification of wealth and soul from impurities after a period of hardship and trial.

## Why Does "Context" Say the First Word in Islamic Dream Interpretation?

### Vital Factors Altering the Interpretation:

* **The Time of the Dream (Dawn, Daytime, Specific Nights of the Lunar Month):** Dreams close to dawn (due to the purity of the air and the tranquility of the soul) are closer to true dreams (Ru'ya al-Sadiqah), whereas dreams during the day or during the initial nights of the lunar month are mostly influenced by daily thoughts and have a different interpretation.
* **The Spiritual State and Purity of the Individual (Having Wudu, Stress, Janabah/Ritual Impurity):** Having ritual purity and Wudu (ablution) at the time of sleep brings the soul closer to the celestial realm and the reception of true inspirations; conversely, sleeping in a state of Janabah or severe stress often stems from satanic whisperings and mental distractions, and holds little interpretive value.
* **Daily Intentions and Preoccupations (Hadith al-Nafs or True Dream?):** If a person has thought deeply about a specific subject during the day, seeing it in a dream is merely "Hadith al-Nafs" (a reflection of thoughts); however, if the dream is seen suddenly and without any prior mental background, it is likely a true dream and an inspiring message.

## Different Scenarios of Seeing Blood in a Dream

* **Interpretation of a dream about vomiting blood:** If the amount of blood is large and fluid, it is a sign of sincere repentance (Tawbah Nasuh), turning back from major sins, or attaining a sudden wealth and fortune; however, if the blood is dark and accompanied by pain, it is interpreted as illness or release from an old grudge.
* **Interpretation of a dream about blood on the ground:** Seeing stains or a pool of blood on the ground usually points to the existence of sedition (Fitnah), hostility, or a sudden crisis in the living environment, and indicates that a wrong decision may cause harm to the family or those around you.
* **Interpretation of a dream about washing blood:** Trying to clean and wash blood off clothes or the body indicates the individual's remorse for past mistakes, a strong desire to compensate for sins, and the cleansing of wealth and life from dubious incomes.
* **Interpretation of a dream about drinking blood:** In Islamic interpretations, eating or drinking blood is strictly forbidden, and in a dream, it is a sign of plunging into forbidden wealth, false judgments, or becoming involved in enmity and a sedition (Fitnah) that does not have a good ending.
* **Interpretation of a dream about blood on the hand:** If the hands are stained with blood, it indicates that the individual is pursuing wealth with great hardship or through incorrect paths; it can also be a symbol of unintended oppression or the violation of the rights of others.

## Recommended Actions After Seeing a Blood Dream

* **Seeking Refuge and Turning to God:** As soon as you wake up from a dream that has caused fear or unrest, recite the dhikr "أَعُوذُ بِاللَّهِ مِنَ الشَّیْطَانِ الرَّجِیمِ" to neutralize the effect of whisperings and mental distractions.
* **Giving Charity (Sadakah):** Giving charity is the most effective way to ward off destined calamities and remove the ominousness of unpleasant dreams; the Holy Prophet (PBUH) states that charity removes calamity and changes an unpleasant destiny.
* **Changing Position and Performing Wudu:** It is recommended that after waking up, you change your side (from the left side to the right or vice versa) and by performing a refreshing Wudu (ablution), distance negative energies from yourself and prepare for prayer or dhikr.
* **Not Retelling the Dream to Others:** In Islam, it is emphasized not to share terrifying or unpleasant dreams with just anyone; because the negative interpretation of others can affect your morale, and it is better to disregard it.
* **Reciting Surah Al-Tawhid, Al-Nas, and Al-Falaq (Mu'awwidhatayn):** Reciting these Surahs before returning to sleep or at the beginning of the day acts like a spiritual fortress, protecting your soul and mind against anxieties and disturbing energies.
