## Snake Dream Interpretation from the Perspective of Great Scholars

### Snake Dream Interpretation According to Ibn Sirin

Ibn Sirin considers the snake to be a sign of a hidden and domestic enemy. According to his interpretation, killing a snake is a sign of victory over the enemy, and the snake fleeing indicates the weakness and defeat of ill-wishers.

### Snake Dream Interpretation According to Imam Sadiq (AS)

Imam Sadiq (AS) considers seeing a snake to have aspects such as sovereignty, a woman, a child, and health. He interpreted fighting a snake in a dream as overcoming enemies in reality.

### Snake Dream Interpretation According to Prophet Joseph (AS)

Prophet Joseph (AS) interpreted the snake as a symbol of the cunning and deception of ill-wishers. According to him, gaining control over a snake in a dream is a sign of attaining benefit, honor, and security.

## Different Scenarios of Seeing a Snake in a Dream and Their Interpretations

* **Snake Biting:** This scenario usually means harm, loss, or betrayal by a hidden enemy. A snake bite warns that you must be more vigilant in your relationships with those around you.
* **Killing a Snake:** This is one of the best scenarios of this dream, which indicates complete victory over problems, hardships, and defeating enemies or rivals in real life.
* **Fleeing from a Snake (or Being Afraid of It):** Contrary to its frightening appearance, fleeing from a snake or being afraid of it in a dream means security, peace, and deliverance from dangers or anxieties that you worry about in your waking life.
* **Talking to a Snake:** If the snake speaks to you in a pleasant tone in the dream, it is a sign of reconciliation with an enemy or acquiring wealth and benefit; but if its tone is harsh, it indicates being deceived.
* **Seeing a Snake in the House:** This dream refers to the presence of a domestic enemy, a jealous person, or an ill-wisher among relatives and family members who lives under the same roof with you or visits your home.
* **Being Swallowed by a Snake:** This scenario is a symbol of gaining control over a major situation, attaining power, sovereignty, promotion in rank, or acquiring significant wealth.

## Different Times of Seeing a Snake Dream and Their Interpretations

* **Snake dream in the early evening:** It usually stems from daily thoughts, holds little validity, and its interpretation takes a very long time to manifest.
* **Snake dream in the middle of the night:** It has a moderate interpretation and indicates hostilities or issues that will occur within the next few months.
* **Snake dream at dawn:** This is the most accurate time for a true dream (ru'ya sadiqa) and gives a completely serious and definitive warning of an imminent danger or enemy.
* **Snake dream after the morning Adhan (call to prayer) and during the day:** The interpretation of this dream is very rapid, and its effects usually manifest on the very same day or the days to come.

## Actions That a Believer Must Take After Seeing a Snake Dream

* **Seeking Refuge in God (Isti'adha):** Immediately after waking up, say three times: "أَعُوذُ بِاللَّهِ مِنَ الشَّیْطَانِ الرَّجِیمِ" to remain safe from the evil of unpleasant dreams.
* **Changing Sleep Position:** Based on the Sunnah of the Prophet (PBUH), if you see a frightening dream, change the direction of your sleep (for example, turn from the left side to the right side).
* **Reciting Surahs An-Nas and Al-Falaq:** Reciting the Mu'awwidhatayn (Surahs An-Nas and Al-Falaq) and Ayat al-Kursi is the best way to ward off affliction and establish spiritual peace.
* **Giving Charity (Sadaqah):** Giving charity wards off destined afflictions; after seeing a dream that has caused you worry, definitely give charity, even if it is a small amount.
* **Not Recounting the Dream to Others:** Refrain from telling a snake dream to just anyone, because recounting an unpleasant dream can attract its negative energy.
* **Performing Prayer and Supplication:** If you saw a very disturbing dream, performing a two-rak'ah prayer and seeking goodness and well-being from the presence of God will completely relieve your anxiety.
