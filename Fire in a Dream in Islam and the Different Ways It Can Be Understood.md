## The Interpretation of Dreaming About Fire from the Perspectives of Great Scholars

**Interpretation of Dreaming About Fire by Ibn Sirin:**

Seeing fire without smoke is a sign of gaining closeness to influential figures or acquiring wealth. However, fire accompanied by thick smoke is interpreted as grief, major problems, and social strife.

**Interpretation of Dreaming About Fire by Imam Ja'far al-Sadiq (AS):**

Fire can symbolize strife, war, illness, or backbiting. On the other hand, if the fire is beneficial and providing light, it is a sign of guidance, the acquisition of knowledge, wisdom, and halal sustenance.

**Interpretation of Dreaming About Fire by Prophet Joseph (AS):**

Dreaming of fire is often interpreted as temptations, worldly challenges, and hostility among people. However, if the fire does not cause you any harm, it signifies victory over enemies and achieving honor and dignity.

## Different Scenarios of Dreaming About Fire

* **Lighting a Fire for Light and Warmth:** This is a highly auspicious dream, interpreted as gaining knowledge and wisdom, being guided toward the right path in life, and finding solutions to complex problems.
* **Extinguishing a Fire:** If you extinguish a fire in your dream, it indicates that you will resolve a strife, dispute, or conflict among your family or friends, restoring peace and tranquility.
* **Burning of Body Parts or Clothes in Fire:** This scenario is interpreted as financial loss, illness, or falling into grief and sorrow. It can also serve as a warning to guard your reputation and behavior in your waking life.
* **A House or Shop Catching Fire:** This indicates the occurrence of severe family disputes, financial crises, or falling into major temptations and challenges in personal and professional life.
* **Consuming Fire:** This has a strictly cautionary interpretation and, according to authentic narrations, is a sign of earning income through unlawful (haram) means, consuming the property of orphans, or facing a disastrous end in worldly affairs.
* **Falling into Fire Without Feeling Burned:** Resembling the story of Prophet Abraham (AS), this dream indicates deliverance from a major danger, victory over enemies, and attaining peace and honor after a difficult period.
* **Seeing Fire Accompanied by Thick Black Smoke:** This scenario signifies massive strife, war, destructive rumors in society, or oppression and tyranny brought upon the dreamer or those around them by powerful individuals.

## Different Times of Dreaming About Fire and Their Interpretations

**Dreaming of Fire in the Final Third of the Night (Near Dawn):** This time belongs to truthful dreams (True Visions). A dream of fire during these hours carries a highly precise, definitive, and imminent interpretation, whether it signifies a blessing or a warning.

**Dreaming of Fire in the Early Part of the Night (Before Midnight):** These dreams are often caused by daily thoughts or satanic whispers; they usually hold no specific interpretation, or their meaning is highly weak.

**Dreaming of Fire During the Day (After Sunrise):** Daytime dreams generally hold little to no interpretative value and are mostly triggered by physical exhaustion, mental distractions, or a heavy stomach.
