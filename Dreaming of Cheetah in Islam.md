## Interpretation of a dream about a cheetah according to Islamic scholars

### Imam Ja'far al-Sadiq (AS)

Hazrat Imam Ja'far al-Sadiq (AS) interprets seeing a cheetah in a dream in three ways: first, a strong and powerful enemy; second, acquiring wealth and benefit that is obtained with difficulty from a king or ruler; and third, fear and dread from a rival or the ruler of the time.

### Ibn Sirin's

Ibn Sirin believes that a cheetah is a base and vile enemy who lacks nobility; if you see that you are eating food with a cheetah, you will be safe from the evil of the enemy, but if the cheetah attacks you or bites you, it indicates that a great harm and injury will reach you from an enemy or an illness.

### Jaber Maghrabi and Prophet Daniel

Prophet Daniel and Jaber Maghrabi consider predatory animals, including the cheetah, to be symbols of cruel, corrupt, and evil-practicing people; Jaber Maghrabi emphasizes that overcoming a cheetah or taming it in a dream is a sign of definitive victory over problems, rivals, and oppressors in waking life.

## Different States of Seeing a Cheetah in a Dream and Their Interpretations

* **Killing or taming a cheetah:** It is a sign of a great victory over enemies, overcoming internal fears, and taking control of a very difficult situation in life.
* **A calm or passive cheetah:** It implies that the rivals or dangers around you are currently quiet, but you must not lose your alertness.
* **A cheetah fighting or attacking you:** It warns of a sudden confrontation, a financial crisis, or intense opposition and enmity from a powerful person in waking life.
* **A cheetah running at high speed:** It reflects acceleration, stress, and the high speed of developments in your life, or it sends you a message that you need to act faster in your affairs.
* **Seeing a cheetah cub (replacing the duplicated item):** It represents the beginning of a new and energetic idea or project that requires your care and attention to grow.
* **The interpretation of a dream about a wounded or dead cheetah:** It means the loss of a rival's power, the end of a threatening period, or unfortunately, the loss of a golden and swift opportunity.
