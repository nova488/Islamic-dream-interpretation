## Interpretation of Dreams About Marriage from the Perspective of Religious Scholars

* **Interpretation of dreams from the perspective of Imam Sadiq (AS):** Imam Ja'far al-Sadiq (AS) interprets seeing marriage in a dream in four aspects of goodness and blessing: an elevation in rank and status, an increase in worldly wealth and property, achieving peace and comfort in life, and the formation of joy and happiness within the family.
* **Interpretation of dreams from the perspective of Ibn Sirin:** Ibn Sirin believes that if someone sees themselves getting married in a dream, they will attain status, wealth, and greatness in the real world to the extent of that spouse's value, beauty, and status in the dream; however, if he marries an unknown woman and does not see her, it could be a sign of the approaching end of life or a sudden change in life.
* **Interpretation of dreams from the perspective of Prophet Joseph (AS):** Prophet Joseph (AS) considers the interpretation of marriage in a dream to be mostly related to social and family relationships, interpreting it as a sign of achieving desires, the opening of closed affairs, peace and friendship among those around, and moving away from loneliness and isolation.

## Interpretation of Having a Dream at Different Times

* **Dream at the beginning of the night (initial hours after Isha):** These dreams are usually due to a heavy stomach, daily fatigue, or a reflection of the same day's thoughts (Hadith al-Nafs), and most of them do not have a specific interpretation.
* **Dream in the middle of the night (midnight until before dawn):** At this time, due to the relative peace of body and soul, dreams are to some extent distant from confusion, but their margin of error is still high, and their interpretation may occur with a long delay.
* **Dream at dawn (the final third of the night until before the Fajr adhan):** This time is the truest and most reliable time for seeing a dream; because the soul is in its purest state, demons are far away, and these dreams are usually interpreted soon and exactly as seen.
