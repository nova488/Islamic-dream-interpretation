## Different States of Seeing a Deceased Person in a Dream

* **Laughing and Joy of the Deceased:** This state indicates the peace and satisfaction of the deceased's soul in the world of Barzakh (the purgatory/intermediary realm) and their good ending. It can also be a harbinger of attaining peace and the resolution of problems for the dreamer.
* **Crying, Sadness, or Sickness of the Deceased:** Indicates an unpleasant condition or the entanglement of the departed soul in the world of Barzakh. This dream usually means a severe request and need of the deceased for good prayers, seeking forgiveness, and the giving of charity or payment of debts by the survivors.
* **Receiving a Gift from the Deceased (They Give You Something):** If that object is good and clean (such as new clothes or delicious food), it is a sign of blessing, an increase in lawful sustenance, and the arrival of goodness and benefit into the dreamer's life.
* **Giving a Gift to the Deceased (They Take Something from You):** Contrary to the previous state, if the deceased takes something from your property or clothes and carries it away with them, it is usually interpreted as financial loss, a decrease in sustenance, or the occurrence of illness and grief.
* **Accompanying and Going with the Deceased:** If in the dream you walk behind the deceased or they take your hand and carry you away with them, interpreters consider it a warning of the approach of the end of life, a severe illness, or a great danger, for the repelling of which it is recommended to give charity.
* **Quarreling or Being Angry of the Deceased:** Indicates the dissatisfaction of the deceased with the behaviors, actions, or decisions of the living person. This dream is regarded as a serious warning so that the dreamer reconsiders their life path and behaviors.
