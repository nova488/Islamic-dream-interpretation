## Dream Interpretation from the Perspective of Great Scholars

### Ibn Sirin

Ibn Sirin believes that crying in a dream indicates joy, the disappearance of the grief and sorrows of waking life, and the arrival of happy days. However, he emphasizes that this interpretation holds true only when the crying is without wailing, screaming, and lamentation; because crying accompanied by mourning can be a sign of calamity.

### Imam Ja'far al-Sadiq (AS)

Imam al-Sadiq (AS) interprets crying in a dream in several favorable ways, the most important of which are the forgiveness of sins, divine mercy, and the resolution of difficulties. From his perspective, the tears that flow in a dream are often a sign of the soul being polished, attaining peace, and achieving relief in real life.

### Prophet Joseph (AS)

In the interpretative approach attributed to Prophet Joseph (AS), crying in a dream means liberation from the bondage of hardships, being exonerated from accusations, and attaining honor after a period of suffering. These tears are, in fact, a symbol of washing away past sorrows and the rising of the sun of success and triumph in the near future.

## Different States of Crying in a Dream

* **Silent and Gentle Crying:** This state is the best type of crying in a dream, and in Islamic culture, it is interpreted as a sign of the rain of mercy, the unlocking of problems (relief/Faraj), and the ending of a period of grief and hardship in waking life.
* **Intense Crying with Loud Voice and Sobbing:** From a spiritual and dream interpretation perspective, this state may reflect internal restlessness, hardship, or a warning of a challenge in waking life; from a psychological viewpoint, it indicates the explosion and release of highly intense nervous tension that has been suppressed during the day.
* **Crying Accompanied by Screaming, Wailing, and Lamentation:** This type of crying usually does not have a positive interpretation, and in traditions, it is interpreted as a sign of calamity, intense sorrow, or a feeling of extreme helplessness and inability in facing real-life problems.
* **Crying out of Joy and Happiness:** If a person sees in a dream that they are crying out of sheer happiness, it indicates achieving a long-awaited desire, a great success, or receiving news that will flood their heart with joy and peace in waking life.
* **Crying out of Fear of God (Awe and Repentance):** This state shows the spiritual awakening and inner purity of the person; its interpretation in Islam is the forgiveness of sins, closeness to God, cleansing the soul from impurities, and attracting divine peace and security into life.
* **Crying over a Dead or Deceased Person:** If a person cries at the bedside of a loved one who has passed away, it is usually a sign of deep longing, a need to seek forgiveness for that person, or a reminder of matters that remained unfinished between the person and the deceased.

## Islamic Etiquette and Solutions After Waking Up

* **Seeking Refuge (Istighatha/Isti'adha):** As soon as you wake up, say the dhikr «أَعُوذُ بِاللَّهِ مِنَ الشَّیْطَانِ الرَّجِیمِ» three times to remain safe from the temptations and anxieties of Satan.
* **Changing Body Position:** In prophetic traditions, it is recommended that if you wake up in a distressed state, change the side you are lying on (for example, if you were on your left side, turn to your right side). This action helps change the flow of energy and calm the mind.
* **Performing Wudu (Ablution) and Praying:** Performing Wudu pours cold water on the fire of internal anxiety. Praying two rak'ahs of recommended prayer (such as the Prayer of Thanksgiving or the Prayer for Warding off Calamity) and seeking refuge in the court of God calms the heart and neutralizes the effect of distressed dreams.
* **Giving Charity (Sadakah):** Giving charity is one of the strongest recommended solutions in Islam to ward off calamity and remove the ill-omened nature or unpleasant interpretations of dreams. By giving charity, guarantee the peace of your heart.
* **Not Recounting the Dream to Just Anyone:** Sorrowful or ambiguous dreams should not be told to everyone. In Islam, it is emphasized to either conceal the dream or recount it only to a wise, well-wishing, and faithful person who will give you a positive and hopeful interpretation.
* **Supplication and Seeking Forgiveness (Istighfar):** Since crying in a dream is sometimes a sign of the soul's awakening, waking up after it is a good opportunity for seeking forgiveness «أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللهَ رَبِّی وَ أَتُوبُ إِلَیْهِ» and asking God for goodness and a good end to affairs.
